The Die Hard jug puzzle

You have a 3-gallon jug, a 5-gallon jug and a fountain. Put exactly 4 gallons on the scale or the bomb goes off. No markings on the jugs, no guessing. Watch the scene on YouTube, then try it yourself.

Your moves

    Stuck? Press Next for a hint

    Next always takes the next move of the outlined route from wherever you are, even after your own moves. Tap a route to switch and start again.

    What the map shows

    1. Each dot is one possible situation: across is the water in A, up is the water in B. The big dot is where you are now.
    2. After any move, one of the jugs is completely full or completely empty. So you can only ever stand on the edge of the map. The hollow dots in the middle are impossible.
    3. Filling or emptying slides you along a side. Pouring moves you diagonally, because the total amount of water does not change.
    4. There are only two sensible ways round the edge: keep filling A, or keep filling B. One of them is always the shortest answer.

    The reason it always works is on the Bézout page: the frog’s jumps are the fills and the empties.
